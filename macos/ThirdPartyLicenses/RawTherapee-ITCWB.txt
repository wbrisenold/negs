RawTherapee ITCWB spectral reference data and method

The ITCWB reference chromaticities bundled at
crates/spektrafilm-io/src/itcwb_reference_xy.bin are derived from the 429
measured reflectance spectra in RawTherapee's rtengine/colortemp.cc. The
candidate search follows the spectral-reference white-balance approach used
by RawTherapee's Illuminant Temperature Correlation white balance (ITCWB).

This is a Rust implementation of the ITCWB spectral-reference method, not a
line-for-line port of RawTherapee's C++ implementation. Its reduced scoring
search intentionally reports failure when a frame lacks sufficient varied
color data, and photographers should review the estimate visually.
